## 🌟 About otherwis3.com

Welcome to **otherwis3.com** (now **lex.games**) - your destination for daily word and logic puzzles that will challenge your mind and keep you coming back for more! Whether you're a casual player or a puzzle enthusiast, we've got something for everyone.

## 🎯 The Games

Our platform features an amazing collection of daily puzzles:

### 🔤 **Conlextions**
Inspired by NYT's Connections - sort 16 terms into four perfect sets of four. Can you find all the connections?

### 🧩 **Lexicogs**
A crossword-style challenge where you assemble answers from provided word chunks. It's like solving a puzzle within a puzzle!

### 💃 **Square Dance**
Solve three jumbled words of varying lengths, each missing one letter. Dance your way through the challenge!

### 🎲 **Six Appeal**
A thrilling Wordle/Mastermind mix! Guess six-letter words - and here's the twist: your guesses don't even have to be real words!

### 🎵 **By A Vowel**
A daily word jumble with missing vowels. A, E, I, O, U... where did they go?

### 📰 **Daily Crosswords**
From mini to full-size crosswords - perfect for your morning coffee or afternoon break!

### 🧠 **Mind Control**
A Mastermind-inspired puzzle that will truly test your deductive reasoning (subscriber exclusive).

### 🔐 **Cryptograms**
Crack the code with letter-substitution puzzles revealing clever quotes and phrases.
